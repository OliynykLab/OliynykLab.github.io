## Highlighted

Our research is special in the sense that we do exploratory synthesis, trying to learn about structure-property relationships of various compositions and crystal systems. Many of these properties are valuable, such as superconductivity, negative thermal expansion, thermoelectric conductivity, neutron absorption, etc. and therefore can afford various benefits to society.

Much of our work should be accessible on an open-access basis. However, if you cannot locate a specific manuscript online, please feel free to contact us!
